Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Shipping Areas

We ship to all 27 Egyptian governorates: Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Qalyubia, Dakahlia, Sharqia, Gharbia, Monufia, Beheira, Kafr El Sheikh, Damietta, Port Said, Ismailia, Suez, North Sinai, South Sinai, Red Sea, New Valley, Matrouh, Fayoum, Beni Suef, Minya, Assiut, Sohag, Qena, Luxor, and Aswan.

Shipping Fees

Shipping is a flat rate of 50 EGP for all orders, regardless of location. Free shipping may be available on orders above a certain amount — check our homepage for current promotions.

Delivery Times

  • Cairo & Giza: 1–2 business days
  • Other governorates: 2–5 business days
  • Remote areas: 4–7 business days

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed on Fridays or public holidays will be processed the next working day.

Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a notification via WhatsApp with your tracking information.

Damaged or Lost Packages

If your package arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us immediately via WhatsApp. We will work with the courier to resolve the issue promptly.

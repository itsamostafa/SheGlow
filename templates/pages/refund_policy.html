Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Return Window

We accept returns within 7 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging.

Return Conditions

  • Item must be in its original, unworn condition
  • Original packaging must be intact
  • Proof of purchase (order number) is required
  • Items purchased on sale are final sale and not eligible for return
  • Customized or personalized items cannot be returned

How to Return

Contact us via WhatsApp within 7 days of receiving your order. We'll provide return instructions and a return authorization. The customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

Refund Method

Approved refunds will be processed within 3–5 business days via the original payment method (InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, or credit). Cash on delivery orders will be refunded via InstaPay or Vodafone Cash.

Defective Items

If you received a defective or incorrect item, please contact us immediately. We'll cover all return shipping costs and send a replacement at no charge.
